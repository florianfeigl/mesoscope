\section{Electronics and Motion Control}
\label{sec:electronics}

\subsection{Compute stack}
The compute stack is a Raspberry~Pi~5 (\SI{8}{\giga\byte}) with the AI~HAT+
(Hailo-8, \SI{26}{TOPS}) on the PCIe interface and a DS3231 real-time clock on
the GPIO header for time-stamping unattended runs. The Hailo driver requires a
DKMS rebuild and a reboot in a specific order (Section~\ref{sec:software}).
The three boards stack inside the pull-out electronics drawer
(Fig.~\ref{fig:drawer}).

\subsection{Focus axis}
The Z-focus axis is driven by a 28BYJ-48 5\,V geared stepper through the
Sangaboard's on-board ULN2003 channel, as in the stock OpenFlexure Z actuator.
Two further 28BYJ-48 motors from the original XYZ plan are on hand as spares.

\subsection{Rail axis}
The linear rail is driven by a NEMA17 stepper with an integrated Tr8$\times$2
leadscrew via a TMC2209 stepstick, with an anti-backlash nut, a coupler and an
end bearing. This axis carries the imaging head between chip stations.

\subsection{Stage driver architecture}
Motion control follows a \emph{hybrid} architecture, decided on
2026-09-03. The Sangaboard is retained for the two tasks it already supports
natively out of the box, namely the focus stepper and the illumination LED (PWM
dimming), so no bare-GPIO focus or LED driver has to be written. The new linear
rail, which the Sangaboard's ULN2003 channels cannot drive, runs on a
standalone TMC2209 stepstick wired to the Pi~5 GPIO (step/dir/enable) and
sequenced from Python. The Sangaboard's now-unused X/Y outputs are left
unconnected, since the tilted geometry makes the original sample-stage axes
functionless (Section~\ref{sec:mechanical}).

\subsection{Illumination driver}
Illumination uses the fibre-coupled mirror-based ModLight source
(Section~\ref{sec:optical}). Because that variant drives one LED channel at a
time, the existing Sangaboard PWM illumination output is retained to power and
dim the active channel---no dedicated driver board is required. The dedicated
ModLight LED controller (Gerber and schematic in
\texttt{sources/modlight/Electronics}) becomes necessary only if simultaneous
multi-channel operation is later adopted; it is kept as a documented fallback.
