\section{Printed Parts and Print Settings}
\label{sec:appendix}

All parts print in PLA (optical parts in black to suppress stray light) at
\SI{0.2}{\milli\meter} layer height (\SI{0.15}{\milli\meter} for the optics
module), \SI{0.4}{\milli\meter} nozzle, three perimeters and
\SIrange{20}{30}{\percent} infill. A brim is recommended for the main body. The
canonical, per-part checklist is \texttt{hardware/PRINT\_CHECKLIST.md};
Table~\ref{tab:printed} lists the custom and must-print parts.

The stock OpenFlexure illumination housing (\texttt{condenser.stl} and the
dovetail arm) is no longer used: it cannot clear the \SI{16}{\milli\meter} chip
and is replaced by the fibre-coupled ModLight source~\cite{Gibson2023}
(\S\ref{sec:optical}), whose design files are mirrored under
\texttt{sources/modlight/} (the OpenFlexure-adapted \texttt{Condenser\_Holder}
and \texttt{Fibre\_Holder} are provided as STL only; the mirror source housing
and common fibre parts also ship as OpenSCAD and STEP).
