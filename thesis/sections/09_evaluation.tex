\section{Evaluation}

\subsection{Cost}
The primary non-functional requirement is a parts cost below \num{500}~EUR
(NFR1, RQ1). Table~\ref{tab:cost} places the mesoscope against the open
OpenFlexure reference builds and a commercial-vendor estimate. The imaging head
as built (kit of parts, reusing an owned ZEISS objective) is
$\approx\num{306}$~EUR; adding the linear-rail motion subsystem brings the full
system to $\approx\num{443}$~EUR, still within the \num{500}~EUR budget. If the
objective had to be bought new at market price the total would rise to
$\approx\num{695}$~EUR, which is the main sensitivity in the budget.
